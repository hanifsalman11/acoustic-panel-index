How many rolls of Mass Loaded Vinyl (MLV) and tubes of Green Glue do I need for 480 sq ft of walls?

Why is 2-inch rigid fiberglass / Rockwool (NRC 1.0) better than 1-inch eggcrate foam?

Cheap 1-inch open-cell polyurethane foam has an NRC of only 0.25–0.40 and only absorbs high-frequency hiss above 2,000 Hz—leaving boxy 250–500 Hz vocal mud untouched. A 2-inch rigid fiberglass (OC 703) or 3-inch Rockwool panel achieves an NRC of 0.95–1.05, absorbing broadband energy down to 125 Hz.
